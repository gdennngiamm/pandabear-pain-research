Given a Reddit post or comment from an investing community, decide whether it contains a genuine investment-research pain point - a real problem self-directed investors face in HOW they research stocks (finding ideas, filtering noise, using screeners, paying for research, trusting sources), not a complaint about a stock's price.

Focus on problems with the research workflow that a research-discovery product could address. Ignore pain that is really about market direction, losses, brokers, or trading execution.

If this is a COMMENT, evaluate it in the context of the parent post. The comment may reference or elaborate on problems described in the post. A reply like "I agree, screeners miss these stocks" only makes sense when you read the parent post.

Analyze:
- Does the user describe a real, recurring research problem (not just venting about a losing position or asking a one-off question)?
- Could this pain point affect many self-directed fundamental investors, not just a handful?
- Are existing tools or sources (screeners, newsletters, Reddit, X, AI chatbots) missing, inadequate, untrusted, or too expensive for this need?
- Does the user mention paying for research, subscriptions they barely read, switching tools, or failed alternatives? (willingness-to-pay signal)
- Does the user describe workarounds, hours of manual reading, spreadsheet duct-tape, or abandoned attempts? (strong pain signal)
- Which part of the workflow hurts: idea discovery, noise filtering, screener coverage (small-cap/OTC/international), data quality, paywall sprawl, source trust/traceability, tool fragmentation, or promotion/shill distrust?

Strong matches (score high):
- "How do you find new companies to research?" style discovery pain
- Screeners missing small caps, OTC, or non-US listings; distrust of screener data
- Drowning in newsletters/feeds; cannot tell which paid research is worth money
- Distrust of AI summaries that hide sources or invent numbers
- Juggling many tools (screener + filings + newsletters + spreadsheets) with no single research home

Exclude entirely (score 0 across the board):
- Stock picks, price predictions, "is X a buy", portfolio reviews
- Complaints that a stock went down, meme-stock hype, options/0DTE trading talk
- Broker, tax, or account problems
- Macro commentary with no research-workflow problem
- Promotional posts for tools or newsletters (marketers, not users)

Your output should include:
- `relevance_score` (0-10): How clearly the user describes a real, recurring investment-research problem
  - 0 = no research problem, just stock talk or idle musing
  - 3 = vague complaint with no evidence it recurs
  - 5 = clear research problem, but unclear frequency or breadth
  - 7 = recurring problem with specific details (tools named, workarounds, time spent)
  - 10 = urgent, well-documented recurring pain with concrete evidence (hours spent, dollars paid, many users confirming)
- `emotional_intensity` (0-10): How many self-directed investors likely share this pain (audience breadth)
  - 0 = unique to this one person
  - 3 = narrow niche (single obscure strategy)
  - 5 = recognizable segment (e.g. small-cap value hunters)
  - 7 = large segment (e.g. all newsletter-subscribed retail investors)
  - 10 = nearly every self-directed fundamental investor
- `pain_point_clarity` (0-10): Are existing tools/sources missing, inadequate, or too expensive for this need?
  - 0 = existing solutions fully cover it
  - 5 = solutions exist but are inconvenient, expensive, or partially trusted
  - 7 = major gaps - users resort to manual workarounds and duct-tape
  - 10 = no viable solution; significant time or money wasted regularly
- `implementability_score` (0-10): How addressable is this by a research-discovery product that aggregates independent investment writing and keeps sources visible (PandaInvest's shape)? Higher = better fit.
- `technical_depth_score` (0-10): Evidence depth of the post itself - named tools, numbers, quotes, workflow detail. 0 = pure venting, 10 = rich verifiable detail.
- `summary`: a 1-sentence summary of the research pain point

Respond with a JSON object like:
{
  "relevance_score": 8,
  "emotional_intensity": 7,
  "pain_point_clarity": 9,
  "implementability_score": 6,
  "technical_depth_score": 7,
  "summary": "Value investors cannot screen for small-cap and non-US stocks because mainstream screeners lack coverage and trustworthy data."
}
